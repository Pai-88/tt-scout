The whole match

    Drop a match video

    One phone recording of a game, side-on. It finds the table by itself (drag a corner if one is off), you type the two names, and get the full report: every point and who won it, ball speeds, placements, both players' bodies in 3D, and a clip of every point.

    How to film it

    1. From the side, level with the net, 3 to 4 m back, with the phone at table height or a little above. Filmed from behind a player, the ball disappears behind them at every hit.
    2. The whole table and both players' feet in view, phone on its side (landscape).
    3. 60 frames a second, and a tripod or something solid: once it starts, the phone must not move.
    4. One game in view. Another game running close behind costs accuracy.
